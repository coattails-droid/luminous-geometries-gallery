Luminous Geometries

Luminous Geometries grows volumetric fields from a single hash. Each token spins its own constellation — 48 accent dots webbed in thread around the core — in a seamless loop. Five palettes, weighted: Sunlight 40%, Moonlight 15%, Midnight 15%, Solar Eclipse 15%, Lunar Eclipse 15%. Motion trait: wave star 50% / spiral 50%. Dots: sparse / standard / dense. Fully on-chain: the renderer, the geometry, and the metadata all live on Ethereum — no servers, no IPFS, nothing to disappear.
