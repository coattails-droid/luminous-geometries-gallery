Luminous Geometries

Luminous Geometries grows volumetric fields from a single hash. Each token spins its own constellation — 48 accent dots webbed in moonlight or sunlight thread around the core — in a seamless 220-second loop. Five palettes, weighted: Sunlight 40%, Moonlight 30%, Midnight 20%, Solar Eclipse 5%, Lunar Eclipse 5%. Motion traits: wave still 40% / double 20% / spiral 40%, fold forward / reverse 50/50. Seamless 220-second loop. Fully on-chain: the renderer, the geometry, and the metadata all live on Ethereum — no servers, no IPFS, nothing to disappear.
