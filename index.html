Luminous Geometries

Luminous Geometries grows volumetric fields from a single hash. Each token spins its own constellation — 48 accent dots webbed in thread around the core — in a seamless loop. Five palettes, weighted: Sunlight 60%, Moonlight 10%, Midnight 10%, Solar Eclipse 10%, Lunar Eclipse 10%. Motion trait: wave still 80% / spiral 20%. Fully on-chain: the renderer, the geometry, and the metadata all live on Ethereum — no servers, no IPFS, nothing to disappear.
