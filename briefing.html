Luminous Geometries

Motion choreography & build notes

From Tidal to Luminous

Luminous Geometries grew out of the Tidal Geometries engine — the same volumetric field renderer, rebuilt from a fresh genesis hash with a new triangle dot grid. Where Tidal mapped orbital ring-lattices across a plane, Luminous turns inward: a central heart-light wrapped in Fibonacci shells, webbed with accent points in thread.

The Motion Choreography

Every token runs a seamless 227-second loop. The camera is locked — a forward-only 360° orbit at 5.625° elevation and 2.2× zoom. No cuts, no reversals. One continuous revolution.

Star is the steady state. The form holds its breath — shells at full brightness, webbing at rest, the heart-light burning at 100%.

Spiral sweeps brightness through the form in rotating arms. The geometry stays locked at the same radius as Star — only light moves.

Form folds the shells out or in at the loop's breath points. A slow inhale, exhale across the full 227 seconds.

The Traits

Four public traits, all driven by the token's hash:

Palette — Sunlight 40%, Moonlight 15%, Midnight 15%, Solar Eclipse 15%, Lunar Eclipse 15%
Wave — Star 50%, Spiral 50%
Points — Sparse, Standard, or Dense accent dot counts
Form — folding out or folding in

Beneath the traits, every token gets its own constellation: hash-seeded dot positions, dual foci, and ring radii. No two are alike.

Fully On-Chain

The renderer, the geometry derivation, and the metadata all live on Ethereum. Each token's seed generates its unique form at render time — no servers, no IPFS, nothing to disappear.
